Tiled 1.0.3 changes what happens when the windows you are tiling over
stay tiled. With two or more windows in the way, they now move to the other
side of the screen instead of being sent back to their old frames — tile a
left half into the right half and the two right-hand quarters trade places
with the left-hand ones, so all three windows keep their tiling.

## Install

1. Download `Tiled-1.0.3.zip` below and move `Tiled.app` to `/Applications`.
2. On first launch, right-click the app and choose **Open** (the bundle is
   ad-hoc signed; there is no Developer ID yet).
3. Grant Accessibility permission when Tiled asks, then relaunch it.

Universal bundle (Apple Silicon and Intel), macOS 13 or later. The attached
`.sha256` verifies the download:
`shasum -a 256 -c Tiled-1.0.3.zip.sha256`.

## What changed

- **Windows in the way keep their tiling.** A slot request blocked by two
  or more windows used to evict all of them: they returned to their
  pre-tile frames, often landing on top of the arrangement you just built.
  Now each window reflects into its own slot on the other side of the same
  layout, mirror image for mirror image. The left half's two right
  quarters become the two left quarters; the two right corner sixths of a
  right two-thirds become the two left ones. Windows land on the other
  side whether or not the window you tiled came from there.
- **All or nothing.** If one of those slots is taken, or the window in the
  way is the middle third or a middle sixth (which mirrors onto itself),
  every window in the way untangles exactly as before — one window moving
  and its neighbour going home is an arrangement nobody could predict.
- One Undo still puts the whole event back: every window returns to the
  slot it held, the requester included, and an expanded window always
  untangles rather than moving.

The single-window rules are untouched: a swap still trades tiles with the
one window in the way, a lone evictee still steps aside into the slot the
request leaves free, and focus still follows a swapped window.
