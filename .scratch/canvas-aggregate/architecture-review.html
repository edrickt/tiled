Problem

One request’s rules are split across plan, PlanExecutor, settle, and reflow; every caller re-derives the restore set, reflow exclusions, and undo chapter.

Solution

One aggregate per canvas owning occupancy, grid, records, expansion, and undo, behind apply(request) → Outcome and undo().

Wins

  • execute’s 11 params collapse
  • Grid returns to its canvas
  • Undo chapters built once
  • Tests stop copying apply order
  • Locality: rules live once

Problem

Ineffective-action feedback is decided by the controller while success is announced unconditionally by the call site, so one click can produce contradictory announcements.

Solution

Wins

  • No contradictory announcements
  • Refusal copy single-homed
  • Feedback folds into Outcome
