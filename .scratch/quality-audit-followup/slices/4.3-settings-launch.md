### S2 (LOW, test gap) — Reset-over-backup overwrite promise is unpinned

- Evidence: `Sources/tiled/SettingsShortcutsTab.swift:731-734` builds the
  "This replaces your previous backup…" promise when a backup already exists;
  the suite asserts only the no-backup promise
  (`Tests/tiledTests/ShortcutsResetBackupTests.swift:163-165`), and no test
  anywhere names the overwrite wording (grep for "replaces your previous"
  across `Tests/` is empty).
- Reachable sequence: Reset Shortcuts (backup parked) → Reset Shortcuts
  again → confirm → first snapshot replaced wholesale. The dialog wording is
  the only guard; a regression to the wrong promise stays green while the
  dialog misleads at the destructive step.
- Direction: extend `ShortcutsResetBackupTests` through the existing
  `confirmReset` seam (`SettingsShortcutsTab.swift:70`) to capture the second
  promise and assert the first snapshot is replaced only after confirm.

### S3 (LOW, UI) — Restore skip message overwrites the registration-failure line

- Evidence: `reload` (`Sources/tiled/SettingsShortcutsTab.swift:238-246`)
  clears the conflict line then re-surfaces registration failures
  (`:284-295`); `restoreResetBackup` calls `reload` (`:803-804`) and then
  overwrites the same single line with "Not restored…" (`:807-814`).
- Reachable sequence: a failed registration is showing beside its recorder →
  press Restore Previous Shortcuts with an externally claimed combo → the
  "Not restored" line replaces the failure line until the next re-read. Both
  truths compete for one announcement line; the failure stays visible in the
  menu, but the tab stops naming it.
- Test gap: no test combines a non-empty `failedActionsProvider` with a
  clash-skipped restore.
- Direction: join both messages on the conflict line, or re-surface failures
  after the restore message, with a combined-case test through the real tab.
